The House

Your own country hideaway

Set on a private property in Landenberg, the carriage house is a restful base for exploring southern Chester County. Wake up to birdsong, enjoy a slow coffee, then head out to world-famous gardens, wineries, mushroom-country farm stands and miles of woodland trails.

Inside, you'll find a comfortable, thoughtfully furnished space with everything you need for a weekend escape or a longer stay — including a well-equipped kitchen, fast Wi-Fi and a cozy place to unwind at the end of the day.

Questions

Good to know

When will my card be charged?

50% of the total is charged when you book. The remaining 50% is charged automatically to the same card 7 days before check-in, and we'll email you a reminder beforehand. If you book within 7 days of arrival, the full amount is charged at booking.

How do I cancel?

Use the "manage booking" link in your confirmation email. Cancel any time before your final payment is due for a full refund. Refunds usually appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.

What taxes are included?

Pennsylvania requires us to collect the 6% PA Hotel Occupancy Tax and the 5% Chester County Hotel Room Rental Tax on stays of fewer than 30 nights. These are shown separately in your price breakdown before you pay.

What counts as a holiday weekend?

Long weekends around US federal holidays — for example Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day and Thanksgiving. These nights are priced at the weekend rate and require a 3-night minimum stay.

Can I bring my dog?

Sorry, we're unable to accept pets of any kind.

Is the carriage house also on Airbnb?

Yes — our calendars are synced, so dates booked on either site are blocked on the other.
